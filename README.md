# 🏥 Hospital Revenue Leakage Analysis
> End-to-End Business Analyst Portfolio Project

[![SQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/SQL-MySQL-blue?logo=mysql)](https://www.mysql.com/)
[![Excel](https://img.shields.io/badge/Excel-Data%20Cleaning-green?logo=microsoft-excel)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/excel)
[![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Power%20BI-Dashboard-yellow?logo=power-bi)](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/)

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Hospitals lose significant revenue due to billing errors, missed charges, and process inefficiencies. This project identifies the root causes of revenue leakage and quantifies the financial impact using real-world healthcare data patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze hospital billing and operational data, detect patterns of revenue loss, and provide actionable recommendations to improve financial efficiency in a healthcare setting.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Excel** | Data cleaning, preprocessing, and initial exploration |
| **SQL (MySQL)** | Querying and aggregating billing records |
| **Power BI** | Interactive dashboard and visual storytelling |

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

- **Billing Error Detection** — Identifying incorrect charge codes and underbilled services
- **Missed Charge Analysis** — Services rendered but not billed
- **Department-wise Leakage** — Revenue gaps broken down by department
- **Payer Mix Analysis** — Insurance vs. out-of-pocket trends
- **Trend Analysis** — Monthly and quarterly revenue patterns

---

## 🔍 Approach & Methodology

1. **Data Collection** — Gathered hospital billing and operational data
2. **Data Cleaning** — Removed duplicates, handled nulls, standardized formats in Excel
3. **SQL Analysis** — Wrote queries to detect leakage patterns and aggregate KPIs
4. **Dashboard Creation** — Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for stakeholders
5. **Insights & Recommendations** — Delivered actionable findings to reduce revenue loss

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Identified significant billing errors in specific departments contributing to revenue loss
- Detected missed charges primarily in outpatient procedures
- Recommended process improvements projected to reduce leakage substantially
- Created a reusable dashboard for ongoing monitoring

---

## 📂 Project Structure

```
hospital-revenue-leakage-analysis/
│
├── data/                    # Raw and cleaned datasets
├── sql_queries/             # SQL scripts for analysis
├── excel_files/             # Cleaned Excel workbooks
├── powerbi_dashboard/       # .pbix dashboard file
└── README.md
```

---

## 💡 Business Impact

This analysis provides hospital finance teams with a clear picture of where and why revenue is being lost, enabling data-driven decisions to recover and protect hospital income.
